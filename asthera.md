1. Company Overview
ASTHERA is a Nigerian space-technology and space-intelligence company focused on transforming satellite, Earth-observation and geospatial data into clear, practical and decision-ready insights for real-world challenges.
ASTHERA's approach is to connect space technology with needs on the ground, particularly in areas such as environmental monitoring, climate resilience, flood-risk intelligence, agriculture and sustainable development.
Rather than simply providing raw satellite data, ASTHERA focuses on interpreting and translating space-derived information into useful intelligence that can support institutions, governments, organisations, businesses and communities in making better-informed decisions.
ASTHERA also works to expand access to space science, technology and innovation through STEM education, outreach, mentorship and youth development.
2. Vision
To become a leading African space-intelligence company using space technology, Earth observation and data innovation to address real-world challenges and contribute to sustainable development.
3. Mission
To transform satellite and geospatial data into practical intelligence and innovative solutions that help organisations understand environmental and societal challenges, make informed decisions and build greater resilience, while developing the next generation of African space and technology talent.
4. Core Goals
ASTHERA's key goals are to:
1.	Develop practical space-intelligence solutions using Earth-observation and geospatial data.
2.	Apply satellite data and emerging technologies to environmental, climate, agricultural and disaster-risk challenges.
3.	Develop innovative tools for flood-risk monitoring and early-warning intelligence.
4.	Support data-driven agricultural and environmental decision-making.
5.	Build partnerships with government institutions, universities, research organisations, private companies, NGOs and international organisations.
6.	Develop young people through STEM, space education, mentorship and practical technology programmes.
7.	Increase awareness and practical use of space technology in Nigeria and across Africa.
8.	Build an innovative African company capable of developing scalable space-technology products and services.

7. How ASTHERA Works
ASTHERA follows a problem-first approach.
Real-world problem → Relevant space/geospatial data → Analysis → Intelligence → Practical decision support
The company seeks to understand the problem first, identify the appropriate data and technology, analyse and interpret the information, and then develop an output that is useful to the intended user.
ASTHERA therefore focuses on usable intelligence rather than data for its own sake.

Bridging the Stars and the Soil — connecting space technology and intelligence with real challenges and opportunities on Earth.
